Sitting Bear
Worsted yarn, 3.5 mm hook, US terms

BODY
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc x6 (12)
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) x6 (18)
Rnd 4: sc in each st around (18)
Rnd 5: (2 sc, inc) x6 (24)
Rnd 6: 2 sc, inc, (3 sc, inc) x5, sc (30)
Rnd 7: sc in each st around (30)
Rnd 8: (4 sc, inc) x6 (36)
Rnds 9–15: sc in each st around (36)
Rnd 16: (4 sc, dec) x6 (30)
Rnd 17: sc in each st around (30)
Rnd 18: (3 sc, dec) x6 (24)
Rnd 19: sc in each st around (24)
Rnd 20: (2 sc, dec) x6 (18)
Rnd 21: (sc, dec) x6 (12)
Rnd 22: sc in each st around (12)
Rnd 23: dec x6 (6)
Stuff firmly, then fasten off and close the last 6 sts.

HEAD
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc x6 (12)
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) x6 (18)
Rnd 4: sc in each st around (18)
Rnd 5: (2 sc, inc) x6 (24)
Rnd 6: 2 sc, inc, (3 sc, inc) x5, sc (30)
Rnd 7: sc in each st around (30)
Rnd 8: (4 sc, inc) x6 (36)
Rnds 9–15: sc in each st around (36)
Rnd 16: (4 sc, dec) x6 (30)
Rnd 17: (3 sc, dec) x6 (24)
Rnd 18: sc in each st around (24)
Rnd 19: (2 sc, dec) x6 (18)
Insert 9 mm safety eyes between Rnds 8 and 9, 8 sts apart, centred on the front.
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff it as you sew it on.

MUZZLE
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc x6 (12)
Rnd 3: sc in each st around (12)
Rnd 4: (sc, inc) x6 (18)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff it as you sew it on.

EAR (make 2)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc x6 (12)
Rnd 3: sc in each st around (12)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. No stuffing needed.

ARM (make 2)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: sc in each st around (6)
Rnd 3: inc x6 (12)
Rnds 4–6: sc in each st around (12)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff lightly before sewing on.

LEG (make 2)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc x6 (12)
Rnds 3–8: sc in each st around (12)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff lightly before sewing on.

ASSEMBLY
1. Sew Head to Body along Rnd 20, centred on the closing.
2. Sew Muzzle to Head over Rnds 10–17, at centre front.
3. Sew both Ear pieces to Head over Rnds 2–8, one on each side, 6 sts from centre front.
4. Sew both Arm pieces to Body over Rnds 12–13, one on each side, 8 sts from centre front.
5. Sew both Leg pieces to Body over Rnds 5–9, one on each side, 3 sts from centre front.
