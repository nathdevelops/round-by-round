Little Red Mushroom
Worsted yarn, 3.5 mm hook, US terms

CAP
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc x6 (12)
Rnd 3: sc in each st around (12)
Rnd 4: (sc, inc) x6 (18)
Rnd 5: (2 sc, inc) x6 (24)
Rnd 6: 2 sc, inc, (3 sc, inc) x5, sc (30)
Rnd 7: sc in each st around (30)
Rnd 8: (4 sc, inc) x6 (36)
Rnds 9–10: sc in each st around (36)
Rnd 11: 3 sc, inc, (5 sc, inc) x5, 2 sc (42)
Rnd 12: (5 sc, dec) x6 (36)
Rnd 13: (4 sc, dec) x6 (30)
Rnd 14: (3 sc, dec) x6 (24)
Rnd 15: (2 sc, dec) x6 (18)
Rnd 16: sc in each st around (18)
Rnd 17: (sc, dec) x6 (12)
Rnd 18: dec x6 (6)
Stuff firmly, then fasten off and close the last 6 sts.

STEM
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc x6 (12)
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) x6 (18)
Rnds 4–10: sc in each st around (18)
Insert 6 mm safety eyes between Rnds 7 and 8, 3 sts apart, centred on the front.
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff it as you sew it on.

ASSEMBLY
1. Sew Stem to Cap along Rnd 15, centred on the closing.
