# Little Red Mushroom

Gauge: 20 sc × 22 rounds = 10 cm (worsted yarn, about a 3.5 mm hook, worked tight).
Finished size: about 8.4 cm tall, cap 9.0 cm across.

Work in continuous rounds; move a stitch marker up each round.

```
CAP (Red, 20 rounds)
Start at the top of the cap with Red.
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: (sc 1, inc) x6 (18)
Rnd 4: (inc, sc 2) x6 (24)
Rnd 5: (sc 3, inc) x6 (30)
Rnd 6: sc 1, inc, (sc 4, inc) x5, sc 3 (36)
Rnd 7: sc in each st around (36)
Rnd 8: sc 2, inc, (sc 5, inc) x5, sc 3 (42)
Rnd 9: (sc 6, inc) x6 (48)
Rnd 10: sc in each st around (48)
Rnd 11: (sc 7, inc) x6 (54)
Rnds 12–13: sc in each st around (54)
Change to Cream.
Rnd 14, in back loops only: sc in each st around (54)
Rnd 15: (sc 7, dec) x6 (48)
Rnd 16: sc 2, dec, (sc 6, dec) x5, sc 4 (42)
Rnd 17: (sc 5, dec) x6 (36)
Rnd 18: sc 1, dec, (sc 4, dec) x5, sc 3 (30)
Rnd 19: (sc 3, dec) x6 (24)
Rnd 20: (dec, sc 2) x6 (18)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Stuff the cap firmly so the dome holds its shape. Leave the centre open for the stem.
```

```
STEM (Cream, 16 rounds)
Start at the base with Cream.
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: (sc 1, inc) x6 (18)
Rnd 4: (inc, sc 2) x6 (24)
Rnd 5, in back loops only: (sc 3, inc) x6 (30)
Rnds 6–8: sc in each st around (30)
Rnd 9: (sc 3, dec) x6 (24)
Rnds 10–14: sc in each st around (24)
Rnd 15: (sc 2, dec) x6 (18)
Rnd 16: sc in each st around (18)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Stuff firmly, pushing stuffing into the base so the mushroom stands.
```

```
SPOTS (MAKE 5) (White, 2 rounds)
With White.
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: (inc, sc 1) x3 (9)
Sl st to join. Fasten off and leave a tail for sewing.
```

## Assembly

- Sew the open top of the stem into the opening on the underside of the cap, stuffing the joint as you close it.
- Pin the spots around the dome before sewing so they look scattered, not in a ring.
- Optional: embroider a few radial lines on the underside in a darker cream for gills.
- For safety eyes, place them on the stem between rounds 10 and 11, about 6 stitches apart.
