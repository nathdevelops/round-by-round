Assembly

    Stitch counts come from slicing each piece every round-height along its surface and dividing the circumference by stitch width. Changes are rounded to 0 or a multiple of 6 so every round splits into even repeats, and increases shift on alternate rounds so the cap stays round. Yarn amounts are a rough estimate.
